# Hybrid per-alliance air tasking

Air tasking uses a separate persistent commander for each active alliance rather than adding air responsibilities to the ground-focused `AllianceAI` or allowing independent sortie generators to consume aircraft. Each alliance air tasking commander owns its doctrine, current mission requests, packages, flights, projected effects, and support-demand history; the existing `AllianceAI` responsibility is renamed Ground Tasking Commander.

Planning is hybrid. On operational cadence boundaries, each air commander rebuilds global mission-request priorities and purges unfulfilled requests from the previous cycle. Every simulation tick, after current campaign effects resolve, a bounded deterministic fulfillment pass evaluates at most eight requests and creates at most four packages per alliance. Dedicated AWACS and tanker requests are fulfilled in a support pass before the combat pass. This was chosen over full theater replanning every tick and cadence-only package creation so air tasking can react organically and use newly available aircraft without unbounded computation or planning churn.

Mission requests express desired effects; persisted packages and flights express commitments. Individual sorties remain the employment of one aircraft and need not be separate planning containers. Active and committed packages contribute projected effects so later cycles do not duplicate inbound attacks or sustained coverage. Package creation reserves individual campaign aircraft, requires a fixed 30-minute preparation delay, and remains deterministic and explainable through stored priority and feasibility diagnostics.
